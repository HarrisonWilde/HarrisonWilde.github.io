I am a statistician working at [Nesta](https://www.nesta.org.uk/) on public health research and policy. I am also affiliated with the [UCL Institute of Health Informatics](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/health-informatics/) and [Turing.jl](https://github.com/TuringLang).

I have previously worked at or been affiliated with [Twitter's Cortex Lab](https://web.archive.org/web/20220529113022/https://cortex.twitter.com/), [Pumas-AI](https://pumas.ai), the [University of Warwick's Department of Statistics](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/statistics/), [Chris Holmes' lab at the University of Oxford](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/), [The Alan Turing Institute](https://www.turing.ac.uk/people/external-researchers/harrison-wilde), [Health Data Research UK](https://www.hdruk.ac.uk/), the [Data Science for Social Good Foundation](https://dssgfellowship.org) and [Homeless Link](https://homeless.org.uk/).
