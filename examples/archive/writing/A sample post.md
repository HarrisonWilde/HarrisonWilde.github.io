This is a sample post. Write it in Obsidian, tick **public**, and it appears on the site, in the feed and in the RSS feed. Until then it's a draft that only you can see in the private build.

## What carries over

Ordinary Markdown works: *emphasis*, [links](https://example.com), lists, quotes and footnotes.[^1] So does ==highlighting==.

> Links to other notes work too: [[Past Lives (2023)]] is public, so it becomes a link, while [[Paris, Texas (1984)]] is private, so only its name appears.

%%
Anything between two lines of double percent signs is private: notes to self, sources, a TODO list. It shows up in the private build and is removed from the public one.
%%

The build refuses to publish if anything private slips through.
